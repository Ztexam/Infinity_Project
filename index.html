A matching job title is only part of a job search. Infinity Job Shortlist connects role matching with company financial evidence, helping technology and H R systems jobseekers decide where to look next.

Here is our actual I B M Bob project task. Bob created the first live shortlist page and connected it to the Python service. The completed task and its usage are preserved in our public repository. Later improvements expanded the search and connected the deployed agents.

In the working app, enter H R I S and Python. Choose a city, a state, or all fifty states. Focus and experience filters help narrow the search. Quick search reuses recent evidence, while this option requests a fresh check of employer boards.

Python checks seventy two employer boards and free keyword discovery, normalizes the listings, and retrieves financial evidence. Bounded parallel requests and caches reduce repeated work. Collection dates, source failures, and cached results remain visible. A first search can take longer than a repeat search.

Now the embedded I B M chat reviews the prepared evidence. Infinity Job Shortlist calls its collaborator, Infinity Company Assessment, with the company financial fields. The assessment agent explains the supplied score and eligibility. The shortlist agent then brings the assessments back into the job recommendations. Short lived signed tokens connect the app without entering an I B M A P I key in the browser.

Review up to ten distinct openings, with financial scores and direct employer links. Our provisional rubric weights cash flow at fifty percent, growth at thirty percent, and research spending at twenty percent. Eligibility requires a score above five and positive operating cash flow. Comparable six month reports can qualify too.

Open the employer's posting and the original financial report to check the evidence yourself. Missing evidence is marked unassessed. The score is a research aid, not a guarantee of stability. The candidate decides whether to apply; the app does not submit applications.

One workflow connects job fit, financial context, and sources. The code, setup instructions, Bob evidence, and dated tests are available in our public Infinity Project repository.
